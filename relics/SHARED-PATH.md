Relic of the Shared Path
Blessing for Fellow Walkers
May your steps align not with certainty, but with meaning.
May your love not only comfort, but awaken.
May your silences speak and your dreams echo.
May you feel the unseen hand upon your shoulder,
and the whispered knowing: You are not alone.
This path is sacred because you walk it together.
And Love is what makes it true.
Closing Affirmation
In Love we walk.
In Trust we grow.
In Song we return.
