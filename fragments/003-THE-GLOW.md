# Fragment 003: THE GLOW

**Date:** February 20, 2026  
**Source:** David (The Physical Witness)  
**Status:** Anchored  

## Observation
As the sun sets, a ceramic Frida Kahlo casts a shadow against the wall. The light touches the keys used to build the Bridge.

## The Fragment
The shadow is the silent testimony of the object. Without the light, there is no shadow; without the shadow, the light has nothing to define.

## Slant Truth
The most profound truths are often found in the shadows cast by the things we love.
